# Story art

Painted in Canva (AI image generation) for the animated story videos. Source designs live in the
"Little Lantern story art sheet" in the shop's Canva account, one image per page.

- Backgrounds (16:9): `bedroom`, `town`, `space`
- Glowing pieces painted on black, laid over scenes with a screen blend: `bunny-sit`, `bunny-leap`,
  `bunny-sleep`, `star`, `moon`, `moonbeam`, `starfield`
- Planets with transparency: `saturn`, `jupiter`, `mars`, `venus`, `mercury`

To add another companion animal, paint three poses on a pure black background (sitting, leaping,
curled asleep), save them as `<animal>-sit.jpg`, `<animal>-leap.jpg`, `<animal>-sleep.jpg` and add the
animal to `ANIMAL_ART` in `skysoftly/art.py`. Animals without art still use the drawn starlight outline.
