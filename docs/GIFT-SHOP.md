# Tonight's Sky, Softly: the gift shop plan

## What we sell

People don't buy "a video". They buy **a bedtime ritual and a keepsake for a child they love**. So the product is a personalised bedtime story pack, and the nebula video is one part of it.

**"___ and the Star That Knew Their Name"**, a personalised bedtime story where the child:
- is the hero, named throughout, with their favourite animal as a companion made of starlight
- travels to **their own nebula**, coloured in their favourite colour and grown from their name
- hears the **real sky of the night they were born**: the Moon's phase, the planets that were up, the constellation overhead, and a star whose light set off decades before they were born
- receives one birthday wish per year of their age
- hears a message from the person who gave the gift, read out by their star
- winds down with slow breathing and a countdown from ten

Every pack contains:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `story.mp3` | The story, about eight minutes, read slowly in the soft British house voice |
| `story.mp4` | A deep zoom into their nebula that lands on a star which bursts into their name |
| `storybook.pdf` | Illustrated 8 x 8 inch storybook: cover, dedication, story pages, their birth-night sky chart |
| `certificate.jpg` | A nebula certificate to print and frame |
| `How to enjoy your gift.txt` | A short note on what's inside |

A finished sample is in `output/stories/sample-isla/`.

## Is there a market?

- **Personalised children's books sell at scale.** Wonderbly has sold over ten million personalised books, with revenue above £30 million a year.
- **Etsy has the buyers.** Personalised bedtime story books sell on Etsy from about $17 to $49. They are printed books, sometimes with a QR code to audio. We found no listing that combines a story, narration, video and the real birth-night sky.
- **Personalised gifting is large and growing.** Research firms put it at roughly $33 to $40 billion in 2026, growing 5 to 10 percent a year. Treat these figures as rough; the firms define the market differently.

## The Etsy listing

**Title**

Personalised Bedtime Story for Kids, Narrated Audio, Nebula Video and Storybook, Birthday Gift from Grandma, Their Real Birth Night Sky

**Tags** (13, each under 20 characters)

personalised story · bedtime story · kids birthday gift · custom name gift · nebula · star map baby · new baby gift · grandchild gift · space nursery · audio story · personalized book · birth night sky · gift from grandma

**Personalisation box**

> Please tell us: 1) child's first name, 2) she, he or they, 3) age they're turning (or "any night"), 4) favourite animal, 5) favourite colour, 6) birth date and town (optional, for the real sky), 7) who it's from, 8) a short message for the star to read (up to 25 words).

**Description, first lines**

> Give them a bedtime story written in the stars. In this personalised story, [Name] is the hero. Their own star calls them to the window, a little animal made of starlight takes them on a moonbeam ride, and they travel to a nebula that belongs only to them. If you share their birth date and town, the story includes the real sky from the night they were born.
>
> You'll receive a narrated audio story (about eight minutes, read softly for bedtime), a video journey into their nebula that ends on a star that turns into their name, an illustrated storybook to print or read on a tablet, and a nebula certificate for their wall.
>
> The narration uses a synthetic (AI) voice. The story text, illustrations and video are generated by our own software from your details.

**Listing images:** `docs/listing/01-hero.jpg` to `04-how-to-order.jpg`. Add a 15-second clip of the name reveal as the listing video.

**Etsy settings**
- List as a **made-to-order digital download**. Etsy then lets you upload each buyer's files after they order.
- Under "Who made it", choose the option for items designed by you, and tick that AI tools were used. Etsy requires AI disclosure, and its 2026 rules reportedly steer AI-assisted items away from "Handmade".
- Etsy limits the size of digital files. Upload the MP3, PDF and certificate directly. Put the video in a Google Drive or Dropbox folder and include the link in a small PDF or in the message.

## Fulfilling an order

1. Copy the buyer's answers into one command:
   ```
   python -m skysoftly story --name "Isla" --pronoun she --age 5 --animal bunny --colour purple \
       --from "Grandma and Grandpa" --message "Happy fifth birthday, darling." \
       --born 2021-05-02 --city Leeds --country GB --video
   ```
2. Listen to the first minute of `story.mp3` and check the name is pronounced well. For unusual names, spell them how they sound in `--name` for the audio, and fix the spelling in the PDF if needed.
3. Upload the files to the Etsy order. Send the video link.

On a 4-core computer the pack without video takes about 4 minutes, and the video about an hour. Run videos overnight in a batch. A faster computer cuts this down a lot.

**Cost per order:** effectively zero. Everything runs on free, offline software on your own computer.

## YouTube, alongside Etsy

YouTube does not pay for mass-produced, template videos, and a channel that posted a birthday video for every name was terminated. So:
- **Do** post long sleep content: one-hour to three-hour "fall asleep inside a nebula" dives, and the nightly "Tonight's Sky" episodes with real sky events.
- **Do** post a few Shorts showing a name reveal, and link to the Etsy shop.
- **Don't** post a video per name.
- Say in descriptions that narration uses a synthetic voice.

## Risks

- **Synthetic voice.** Some buyers want a human voice. A premium tier where you record the story yourself would answer that.
- **Name pronunciation.** The voice can mispronounce unusual names. Always check before sending.
- **Etsy AI enforcement.** Etsy has removed listings over AI disclosure. Disclose clearly in the listing and the settings.
- **Not an official star name.** The certificate says it is a keepsake, not an official astronomical name. Keep that wording.
